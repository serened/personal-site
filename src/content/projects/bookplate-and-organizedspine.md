These two are the reason I left nursing. Bookplate and OrganizedSpine are inventory tools for book people, built on one shared codebase so the personal and the professional sides stay in step.

## What they are

- **[Bookplate](https://bookplate.app)** is for personal libraries: shelves, a reading journal, lending, and a wishlist. It grew out of wanting to actually know what I owned and what I had read.
- **[OrganizedSpine](https://organizedspine.com)** is the same core tuned for independent bookstores: stock and conditions, pricing, and bulk import, built for shops working with small margins.

## Where it's headed

Bookplate launched with the help of a small crew of beta readers. I am heads-down on the work that comes after a launch: paid plans, the rough edges, and the features booksellers keep asking for.
