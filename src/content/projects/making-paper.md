I have always wanted to make something from nothing, and paper is the closest thing I have found to alchemy. I tear up scrap paper, soak it, blend it into pulp, and coax it into sheets by hand. It is messy and slow and I love it.

## What I've tried so far

The first few attempts came out lumpy and thick, more like felt than paper. I have since learned that the secret is mostly in the water — too much and the sheet falls apart, too little and it clumps. I am still calibrating the pour and the press.

## Notes

The end goal pairs with the [bookbinding](/projects/bookbinding/) I am learning and the [dried flowers](/projects/drying-flowers/) I am pressing: a book that is mine end to end, from pulp to cover, with flowers pressed into the pages.
