Making paper is the closest thing I can do that feels like alchemy. There is immense joy in collecting paper towel and toilet paper rolls, tossing them into a blender after a soak, and coaxing them into sheets by hand. It is messy and slow and imperfect.

## What I've tried so far

The first few attempts came out lumpy and thick, more like felt than paper. I have since learned that the secret is mostly in the water — too much and the sheet falls apart, too little and it clumps. I am still calibrating the pour and the press and am attempting to use sheets of felt to help the drying process.

## Why bother?

The end goal of beautiful imperfect paper pages pairs with the [bookbinding](/projects/bookbinding/) I am learning and the [dried flowers](/projects/drying-flowers/) I am pressing: a book that is mine end to end, from pulp to cover, with flowers pressed into the pages. If nothing else, this is a fun way to recycle cardstock.
